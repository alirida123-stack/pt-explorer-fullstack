Fun and Fascinating Facts About Elements

Discover amazing facts about the elements that make up everything in our universe!

🌟 Gold

Gold is so malleable that one gram can be stretched into a wire 2 kilometers long! It’s also one of the few metals found naturally in pure form.

🔥 Hydrogen

Hydrogen makes up about 75% of the universe’s elemental mass — it’s the fuel that powers the stars, including our Sun.

💨 Helium

Helium is so light it escapes Earth’s atmosphere — once released, it drifts off into space forever!

⚛️ Uranium

Uranium is a naturally radioactive element used as fuel in nuclear reactors to generate electricity.

🧊 Mercury

Mercury is the only metal that remains liquid at room temperature. It was once used in thermometers and barometers.

💎 Carbon

Carbon atoms form the basis of all life — and the same element makes up both graphite (soft) and diamond (the hardest natural substance).

💡 Did You Know?

The periodic table is organized by atomic number — the number of protons in each element’s nucleus.

There are currently 118 confirmed elements, and new ones are occasionally discovered in laboratories!

Elements are grouped into categories like metals, nonmetals, and metalloids — each with unique chemical behaviors.

Groups are the vertical columns. Elements in a group share similar properties due to similar valence electrons.

Periods are the horizontal rows. As you move across a period, atomic number increases and trends change predictably.

There are 118 confirmed elements as of today. Most are naturally occurring; some are synthesized in labs.
